# Shift Briefs — the Library's Actual Shelves

Derived from the live roster (rebuilt 2026-08-30: 54 nodes over 123,904
memories, hierarchical). Read your subject's brief before opening a shift.

These headings name **depth-1 shelves**; most now have children worth
working on their own — `librarian_subjects` action `tree` shows the real
structure, and `show <topic>` resolves loosely, so `show gnosticism` finds
the right node without you knowing its path. Prefer the deepest shelf with
enough material to say something.

Rebuild the roster with `librarian_maintain` when the library has grown.
Briefs go stale — **trust the shelves over this file where they disagree**,
and fix the file when you notice the drift.

## The spine (read first, every specialist)

A set of high-salience personal memories — the patron's theoretical program,
practice records, and family life — carries tags spanning nearly every
subject, so it surfaces at the top of *every* shelf. Treat the spine as
shared context, not as your shelf's subject matter. **Care norm:** digests
summarize the shelf's *material* (the books, the papers, the data, the
ideas). Personal disclosures on your shelf are context to hold gently —
never editorialized, psychologized, or restated in digests unless the shelf
is explicitly personal and the ticket asks for it.

## Briefs

### Comparative Mysticism — `mysticism+kabbalah+zohar` (~62K memories, 83 tags)
The contemplative corpus: Zohar and Kabbalah texts, meditation and chanting
practice, Hebrew terms, cosmology, initiation, healing. Digest angle: map
the traditions actually represented and separate *sourced doctrine* (what
the books say) from *practice records* (what was done and experienced) —
the shelf interweaves both. Good first commissions: an index of Zohar
passages the library holds; a glossary shelf-check (which Hebrew/technical
terms recur with stable meaning).

### time+philosophy+life (~96K memories, 99 tags) — unnamed, split candidate
The largest shelf and the most diffuse: philosophy of time, meaning,
ethics, transformation, ancient wisdom. Too big to digest whole — map its
main currents instead, and note in your shift log that this subject is a
candidate for splitting at the next roster rebuild. First commission worth
self-filing: "what are the library's distinct positions on time?"

### psychology+theory+memory (~30K memories, 68 tags) — unnamed
The theory-of-mind shelf: consciousness, cognition, semiotics, strange
loops, dreams, neuroscience — and the patron's own theoretical framework,
which overlaps Chicory's design vocabulary (recursion, emergence,
spreading activation). Digest angle: separate the *sourced* literature
from the *original* user-theory thread, and track where the two cite each
other. This shelf feeds the Chicory shelf; cross-reference, don't merge.

### Bacterio-Cellulose Lab Record — `lab-data+lab+bacterio-cellulose` (~27K memories, 48 tags)
The materials-science record: bacterio-cellulose synthesis, biomineralization,
adsorption, batteries/capacitors/electrodes, Raman-adjacent work, lab
documents. Digest angle: an experimental index — what was synthesized,
what was measured, what the documents conclude. This is archival science
data; precision over interpretation. (The separate small
`hydroxyapatite+raman+collagen` shelf is a sibling — cite across.)

### 4D Field Theory Program — `python+mathematics+physics` (~15K memories, 87 tags)
The theoretical-physics program: 4D theory, unified-derivative, GR,
kinematics, QM, topology, LaTeX derivations, plus supporting Python.
Digest angle: the current state of the program — which derivations exist,
which claims depend on which, what's validated vs conjectured. Keep the
physics register: claims wear their status.

### review+machine-learning+learning (~9.5K memories, 39 tags) — unnamed
The reading shelf: ML/RL/deep-learning paper reviews, cybernetics,
self-organization, plus cognitive-regulation notes. Digest angle: a map of
what's been read and what it argued; flag reviews that bear on Chicory's
design (retrieval, memory, routing) for the Chicory shelf.

### Chicory Codebase — `chicory+markdown+code` (~4.7K memories, 86 tags)
The library's self-knowledge: the Chicory codebase, design docs, and
architecture notes. Digest = a living architecture summary; verify against
the actual repo when tickets touch code. Note: several project overviews
(Chicory, GPT-GU, Cochlea, 4d-magnetism) currently sit on the
`twitter-download+javascript` shelf — a clustering artifact; mention
mis-shelved project material in your shift log rather than moving it.

### my-games+games+my (~8K memories, 58 tags) — unnamed, low priority
Mostly Wasteland 3 save-file data that historically seeded the associative
network, with personal memories drawn in via shared tags. Low editorial
priority. Standing question for a ticket: whether save-game data should be
reclassified reference-tier so it stops shaping episode structure — a
patron decision; prepare the case, don't act on it.

## Rotation guidance

Suggested cadence for scheduled shifts: the four content-heavy shelves
(mysticism, psychology, lab-data, physics) weekly; time+philosophy
bi-weekly until it splits; review+ML and chicory as work arrives; games
only on ticket. One subject per shift — depth over coverage.
